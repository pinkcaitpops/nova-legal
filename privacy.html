Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 5, 2024

1. Introduction

Nova ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our Nova notebook application.

2. Information We Collect

Local Data (Stored on Your Device Only):

  • Notes, notebooks, and tags you create
  • App preferences and settings
  • Search history within the app

Data Processed by Third-Party Services:

  • When you use AI features (summarization, continue writing, etc.), your note content is sent to OpenAI's API for processing
  • If you provide your own OpenAI API key, requests are made directly to OpenAI
  • If using our proxy service, requests route through our Cloudflare Worker

We Do NOT Collect:

  • Personal identification information
  • Email addresses or account credentials
  • Location data
  • Analytics or tracking data
  • Your notes are never stored on our servers

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide AI-powered features (summarization, writing assistance, semantic search)
  • To improve app functionality and user experience
  • All note data remains on your device unless you explicitly use AI features

4. Third-Party Services

  • OpenAI: Powers AI features. Subject to OpenAI's Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat: Manages subscriptions. Subject to RevenueCat's Privacy Policy
  • Apple: App distribution and payments. Subject to Apple's Privacy Policy

5. Data Security

  • All notes are stored locally on your device
  • API keys are stored in secure device storage
  • We do not have access to your notes or personal data

6. Children's Privacy

Nova is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy in the app.

8. Your Rights

You can delete all your data at any time by uninstalling the app or using the "Reset Data" option in Settings.
